About My City

s Bhopal is the capital city of Madhya Pradesh. It is known as the City of Lakes because it has many natural and artificial lakes. The city has a rich history and was ruled by Begums. The Upper Lake is very large. Bhopal is one of the cleanest and greenest cities in India. The chemical formula of water in lakes is H2O and area is 2852 km.
